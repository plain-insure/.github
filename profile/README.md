# Plain

[Plain](https://plain.insurance/en/) makes business insurance simple, transparent, and flexible for companies in Denmark. We help startups, small businesses, and self-employed people get the coverage they need, without fees or fine print.

We are building a modern insurance company in the open where we can. The tools, libraries, and learnings we publish here are part of that commitment. Explore, use, and contribute to the projects that help make business insurance easier to understand and manage.

## Business insurance, made plain

Plain offers digital business insurance that adapts as your company changes. Create an account, answer a few questions, and get tailored coverage in minutes.

- [Industrial injury insurance](https://plain.insurance/en/products/arbejdsskadeforsikring/)
- [General liability insurance](https://plain.insurance/en/products/erhvervsansvarsforsikring/)
- [Business contents insurance](https://plain.insurance/en/products/losoreforsikring/)
- [All business insurance coverages](https://plain.insurance/en/products/)

Plain Insurance ApS is authorised and regulated by the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority. Learn more about [Plain](https://plain.insurance/en/about-us/), browse our [knowledge base](https://plain.insurance/en/knowledge/), or [get business insurance](https://app.plain.insure/start).
